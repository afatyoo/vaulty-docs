Vaulty is a web app for tracking and managing personal or family finances. You can record income and expenses, set monthly budgets, follow bills and savings, and see the whole picture in one place.

## Who it is for

- **Individuals** who want to know where their money goes.
- **Families** managing money together in one organization (Family plan, up to 5 users).
- **Technical users** who want to record and read data through an AI assistant (see [AI agents (MCP)](/en/agen-ai/menyambungkan-mcp/)).

## What you can do

- Record income and expenses with categories and payment methods.
- Attach receipt photos (Personal plan and above).
- Set a budget per category every month.
- Follow recurring bills, savings, goals, and debts (Personal and above).
- See the dashboard, insights, financial health score, and PDF or Excel reports.
- Use the app on your phone as an installed app (PWA).

## Two languages

Vaulty is available in **Bahasa Indonesia** and **English**. One screen uses one language only, including emails, PDF reports, and invoices. Change it in **Settings**.
