Support is provided **by email** at **halo@vaulty.id**, during working hours: **Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 WIB** (Western Indonesia Time). Messages outside those hours are answered on the next working day. We do not offer phone support.

To get help faster, include:

- your Vaulty account email,
- the invoice or order number (for payment matters),
- a short description and, if you have one, a screenshot.

Payments from **outside Indonesia**, **refund** requests, and anything else outside these guides also go to the same email.
