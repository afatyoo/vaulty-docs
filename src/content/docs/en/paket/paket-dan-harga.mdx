## Trial

Every organization gets **one 14-day trial** (Personal plan) with no card. If the trial ends without payment, the account moves to the Free plan and your data stays safe.

## Upgrading and downgrading

- **Upgrades** apply right away. You only pay the **price difference** for the rest of the period.
- **Downgrades** apply at the end of the current period.

## Renewal

Subscriptions **do not renew automatically**. We send email reminders before the period ends. After it ends there is a **7-day grace period** during which you can still read and download your data. New changes are locked until you subscribe again.
