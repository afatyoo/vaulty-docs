## How to pay

Open **Subscription**, choose a plan and period (monthly or yearly), then click **Pay now**. You are taken to a secure payment page processed by **Midtrans**. The available methods (for example QRIS, virtual account transfer, cards, e-wallets, and retail stores) appear on that page. Vaulty never stores your card data.

Only the **organization owner** can pay.

## The payment page

After you click **Pay now**, Vaulty opens a full-screen payment page. **On the left** is the order summary: total, plan, period, subtotal, and order number. **On the right** are the Midtrans payment options, with no popup. The **Check status** button underneath re-checks your payment if the page has not changed.

Once the payment is processed the page switches by itself to **Payment received**, **Waiting for payment** (for transfers or store payments you have not made yet), or **Payment did not go through**.

## After paying

- Your subscription activates automatically once we receive the payment.
- You receive an **email receipt** at your account address, with the **PDF invoice** attached.
- You can also download the invoice any time from **Subscription > Payment history**.

If a payment fails, expires, or is canceled, you receive an email and your subscription does not change. You can create a new order any time.

## Billing details on the invoice

On the Subscription page you can fill in a **billing name, email, address, and tax ID** (optional). These appear on later invoices.

:::caution[An invoice is not a tax invoice]
A Vaulty invoice is **proof of payment for the subscription**, not a tax invoice (faktur pajak).
:::

## Payments from outside Indonesia

Prices follow the **billing country** in the **Billing details** card on the Subscription page. If you have not set it, the country is guessed from your location.

| Plan | Indonesia | Other countries |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | Rp25,000/month, Rp250,000/year | $3/month, $30/year |
| Family | Rp65,000/month, Rp650,000/year | $7/month, $70/year |

USD prices are paid in **rupiah** at that day's exchange rate, rounded up to the nearest Rp1,000. The rate and amount are locked to the order, so the charge does not change while the order waits for payment. The rate used is shown on the payment page and on the invoice.

Pay with a **Visa or Mastercard credit or debit card**, including cards issued outside Indonesia. Your card issuer may add a currency conversion fee. If your card is declined, **email us** and we will help.

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled **case by case**. Email us with your invoice number.
