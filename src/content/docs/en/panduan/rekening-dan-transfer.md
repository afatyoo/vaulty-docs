The **Accounts** menu shows how much money sits in each place: bank, e-wallet, cash, credit card, investments, and more. It is available on every plan. The **Free** plan allows **2 active accounts**.

## How the balance is worked out

You do not keep typing the balance. Vaulty calculates it from your records:

> opening balance + income − expenses + transfers in − transfers out + adjustments

Transactions dated **before** the opening date are not counted, so enter the balance you see in your bank app on that date.

Each account card also shows a **small 30 day balance chart**, with how much it went up or down.

## Add an account

1. Open **Accounts** and click **Add account**.
2. Fill in **Name** (for example BCA, GoPay, Cash), **Type**, **Opening balance**, and **As of**.
3. Save.

The account name appears at the top of the **Method** list when you record income or an expense. Older transactions whose method matches the account name are counted automatically.

If you have been recording for a while, **Methods you have used** offers a **Make it an account** button. A method does not become an account on its own, because some are only ways to pay (for example QRIS or Debit card).

An account that has been used cannot be deleted, only **archived**. Renaming an account is safe: its transactions follow the new name.

## Transfers between accounts

Moving money from BCA to GoPay is neither income nor an expense, so it does not change your reports.

1. Click **Transfer**.
2. Pick the **from** and **to** accounts, then fill in the amount, date, and note.
3. If there is an admin fee, fill in **Fee**. It is recorded as an expense in the category **Biaya Admin** on the source account.

### Transfer suggestions

If you recorded "GoPay top up" as an expense on BCA and as income on GoPay, Vaulty offers to merge them into one transfer. The rule: same amount, different accounts, dates at most 4 days apart. The two old records go to the **trash for 30 days**.

## Match a balance

If the balance in Vaulty differs from your bank app:

1. Click **Match balance** on the account card.
2. Type the real balance.
3. The difference is recorded as an **adjustment**, not as income or an expense, so your reports stay clean.

## Credit cards

Choose the type **Credit card**, then fill in the **limit**, **statement day**, and **due day**. The card shows what is used, the remaining limit, and the percentage used. Insights warn you when the due date is 5 days away and a balance remains, or when 80% or more of the limit is used.

## Monthly statement archive

The **Personal** plan and above can keep the bank's PDF statements on each account.

1. Click **Statements** on the account card (not available for cash accounts).
2. Pick the month and **Upload** the PDF (up to 10 MB).
3. Full months without a statement are shown as quick upload buttons.

An account keeps one file per month. Uploading again for the same month replaces the old one.

## Sharing per account

On the **Family** plan, owners and admins can limit an account to selected members. See [Members and roles](/en/panduan/anggota-dan-peran/).
