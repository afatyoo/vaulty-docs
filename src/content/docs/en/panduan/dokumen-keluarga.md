The **Documents** menu (**Personal** plan and above) keeps your family's important files in one place: insurance policies, vehicle papers, certificates, warranty cards, and the like.

## Upload a document

1. Click **Upload document**.
2. Choose a **PDF or image** (up to 10 MB). Vaulty checks that the content matches the file type.
3. Fill in the **title**, **type** (insurance, vehicle, property, warranty, identity, or other), and the **expiry date** if there is one.
4. Optionally **link** it to an asset (in Net worth) or a bill.

An organization can keep up to **200 documents**.

## Reminders

A document with an expiry date is reminded **30 days** and **7 days** before it expires, through in-app notifications. A notice at the top of the page counts documents that expire within 30 days or already have.

## Download all files

**Owners** and **admins** see a **Download all files (ZIP)** button. The ZIP holds the family documents, account statements, and receipt photos under readable names. Files are not part of the JSON backup so that the backup stays small, so this ZIP is its companion.

## Careful with identity documents

Keep identity documents only if you really need to. Files are stored on Vaulty's servers and can only be opened by organization members who have access.
