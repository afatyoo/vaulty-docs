The **Crypto** menu (**Personal** plan and above) shows market prices, charts, and the value of the crypto you record. Vaulty only **records and shows values**. It has no wallet or exchange connection and gives no buy or sell advice.

## Market and charts

- The **market list** on the right has 20 coins: BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, DOGE, TRX, TON, DOT, LTC, SHIB, AVAX, LINK, POL, XLM, BCH, and ATOM. Use the search box to filter, then click a coin to see its chart.
- The **chart** can be a **Line** or **Candles** (candlesticks with volume). Choose **1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 1Y**, or **All**.
- Drag the chart to the left to see older data. The mouse wheel still scrolls the page.
- Below the chart you see the 24 hour high and low, volume, and the 7 day change.

Prices come from **Indodax** in rupiah and are converted to your display currency. The page refreshes prices every **15 seconds** while the tab is open. Charts are drawn with TradingView Lightweight Charts.

## Record crypto you hold

1. In **Crypto assets**, click **Add asset**.
2. Pick the **Coin**, enter the **Amount of coins**, and (optionally) the **Buy price per coin** and a note.
3. Save.

Each asset appears as a card with its current value, the number of coins, profit or loss against your buy price, and a small 7 day chart. Enter a buy price if you want profit or loss calculated, and leave it empty if you do not know it. Up to 50 assets.

## Portfolio chart

The **Your portfolio** chart shows total value over time. The figures are an **estimate**: the coins you record now times the price at each point in time. Past buys and sells are not included because Vaulty does not record that history.

## How it connects to net worth

Crypto value counts toward **Net worth** on the Dashboard and in [Planning](/en/panduan/perencanaan/), as part of your assets.
