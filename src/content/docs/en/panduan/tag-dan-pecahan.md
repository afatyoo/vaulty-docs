## Tags

Tags group transactions **across categories**, for example "Bali trip" or "Renovation". A transaction can have several tags, and a tag can be used on both income and expenses. Available on every plan.

1. When you record or edit a transaction, pick tags in the form, or type a new name to create one.
2. Tags show as small chips in the table.
3. To filter, open **Filter** on the Income or Expenses page and pick a tag.

The **Tags** menu (analysis section) shows total income, expenses, and the difference per tag. Those totals leave out transactions in accounts that are restricted for you.

## Splitting an expense

One supermarket receipt often mixes food, cleaning supplies, and entertainment. Split it so each part lands in the right category.

1. In the expenses table, choose **Split** on the transaction.
2. Create **2 to 10 parts**, each with a category and an amount. All parts must add up **exactly** to the original amount.
3. Save.

The original expense is **replaced** by its parts, and the original goes to the **trash**. That is why reports, budgets, and the dashboard never count it twice. The parts can be **merged back**; the category follows the largest part.

An expense linked to a bill or to savings cannot be split.
