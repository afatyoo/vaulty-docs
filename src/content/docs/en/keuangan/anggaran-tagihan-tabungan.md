## Budgets

In **Budget**, set a limit per category for each month. Vaulty compares it with your spending and flags categories that went over. The **rollover** option carries the leftover budget to the next month. Free plan: up to 3 categories.

## Recurring bills (Personal and above)

Record monthly bills such as electricity or internet with their due dates. When you mark one as paid, the payment is recorded as an expense.

## Savings and goals (Personal and above)

- **Savings:** record deposits and withdrawals per account (savings or investment).
- **Goals:** set a target with an amount and date, then link it to a savings account to see progress.

## Planning and debts (Personal and above)

In **Planning** you record debts and installments with their payments, and set **email reminders** (bills due, budget nearly used, installments).
