## Dashboard

The dashboard cards show the period balance, cash flow, recent transactions, spending by category, and budget usage. Change the period with the month picker at the top.

## Insights

- **Basic insights** (all plans): where your money goes, by category.
- **Advanced insights** (Family plan): additional analysis.

## Financial health score and heatmap (Personal and above)

The **health score** rates your finances from several components and suggests how to improve. The **heatmap** shows when your spending is highest.

## Reports (Personal and above)

Download a full report as **PDF** or **Excel** for your records or to review with your partner. The report language follows the language you use in the app.
