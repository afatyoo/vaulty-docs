## Income and expenses

Open **Income** or **Expenses** in the menu and click the add button. Fill in:

- **Date**,
- **Source** (income) or **Name** (expense),
- **Category** and **payment method**,
- **Amount** in Rupiah,
- **Note** (optional).

Categories and payment methods come from **Master data**. You can add, edit, or delete them there.

## Receipt attachments

Personal plan and above can attach proof to an expense:

- **Take photo:** uses the rear camera (shown on touch devices).
- **Upload file:** JPG, PNG, WebP, or PDF.

Large photos are shrunk automatically to fit the 5 MB limit.

## Free plan limits

The Free plan is limited to **50 transactions per month** (income and expenses combined) and **3 budget categories**. Paid plans have no such limit.

## Deleting and restoring

Deleted transactions go to the organization's trash so they can be restored.
